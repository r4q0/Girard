SALESBOOK: Matchly (fictional demo company)

== COMPANY ==
Matchly: automatic invoice matching for small and mid-size businesses.
Reads incoming invoices, matches to purchase orders and bank payments, flags mismatches.
For: finance/ops teams, 5 to 100 staff, 200+ invoices/month.
Core value: cuts manual matching ~80%. Fewer late payments, fewer errors.

== PRICING ==
Starter: EUR 250/month, up to 500 invoices/month.
Growth: EUR 400/month, up to 2,000 invoices/month. Most chosen.
Scale: custom, 2,000+ invoices/month.
Setup: EUR 500 one-time. Can waive on annual contract.
Annual contract: 2 months free.
Allowed discount: max 10% extra, only annual, only with manager OK.
Never: free months beyond annual deal, price below Starter.
Free trial: 14 days, own data.

== IDEAL CUSTOMER ==
Fit: 200+ invoices/month, manual matching in Excel, uses Exact Online / Twinfield / AFAS.
Non-fit: under 50 invoices/month, no accounting software.
Disqualify: needs on-premise install. Needs non-EU data storage.

== DISCOVERY QUESTIONS ==
(opening) How do you match invoices today?
(opening) How many invoices per month?
(pain) How many hours a week goes into matching?
(pain) What happens when a mismatch slips through?
(decision) Who else decides on this?
(decision) What does your buying process look like?
(timing) What would make this a priority this quarter?
