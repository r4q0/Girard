SALESBOOK: Koref

== COMPANY ==
Koref: software engineering firm, Amsterdam. Automation, custom software, practical open-source AI for business operations.
Positioning: practical, anti-hype. Reliable, maintainable software with measurable financial return.
Most wasted time needs no AI. Usually two systems not talking. Fix is software, not a model.
Where AI helps: open-source models, Dutch-hosted, infra client or Koref controls.
Fixed price before start. Client owns code. Koref takes operational liability after go-live (hosting agreement).
Main message: "Buy outcomes, not hours."
Promise: automation pays for itself within 18 months, guaranteed.
Values: stands behind work, data before opinion, engineers not marketers, practical over hype, honest (says when nothing worth building), client owns data and system, no lock-in, fixed and fast (weeks, not quarters).

== SERVICES AND PRICING ==
Automation (main): free 30-min discovery call -> workflow assessment EUR 500 fixed (2 to 3 hrs staff time) -> report within 2 days: cost per process, fastest payback, fixed quote per automation. Report is theirs to keep -> build at fixed price, scope, date. 18-month guarantee.
Demo to production: for fast-built (vibe-coded) apps/AI demos going live. Fixes auth, silent errors, DB failing past ~100 users, unclear data storage, no monitoring. Discovery call -> code review within 48 hrs -> hardening -> handover with docs. Price: fixed quote after code review. No standard price.
AI literacy training: EU AI Act Art. 4, since 2 Feb 2025. Process mapping, then 4-hr session on site, up to 15 people, own documents. Written record for the obligation. EUR 2,000 fixed. Over 15 people: trained staff train colleagues, no extra cost. Client may record the session for internal use only, not for distribution. Cross-sell only, not main close.
Hosting and management: from EUR 200/month excl. VAT, monthly billing, 12-month minimum. Compute at cost. Proactive patching, free fixes, Koref liable. Dashboards, direct line. Software Koref did not build: EUR 500 assessment first, then fixed quote. After 12 months: stay, take in-house, or move.

== GUARANTEE ==
Build pays for itself within 18 months, or Koref pays back the difference.
Example: pays EUR 15,000, saves EUR 13,000 in 18 months, Koref pays back EUR 2,000.
Savings measured against baseline agreed in assessment, written into proposal.

== LIVE COST CALCULATION ==
Yearly cost = items/day x minutes/item / 60 x loaded hourly cost x working days/year.
Default 230 working days. Use prospect's own hourly cost. Unknown: ~EUR 48/hr Dutch average, fully loaded, say it is an average.
Example: 3 hrs/day x EUR 48 x 230 = EUR 33,120/yr, ~EUR 2,760/month.
Rep says number back, prospect corrects it. Corrected number = believed number.
EUR 10,000+/yr: supports a build. Under ~EUR 7,000/yr: build unlikely to meet guarantee. Suggest training, simpler fix, or honest "not worth building".
Hidden costs: error correction, overtime, unfillable vacancy, volume growth.

== IDEAL CUSTOMER ==
Fit: Dutch SME, 10 to 250 staff, office/admin team, 2+ systems not connected, growing or struggling to hire admin staff, owner/director/ops manager reachable.
Good assessment prospect: one process with agreed yearly cost, decision maker on call or named, reason to act soon, gives 2 to 3 hrs staff time and system access.
Not a fit: insists on hourly billing (buying capacity, not outcomes). Yearly cost too small.

== DISCOVERY CALL (30 min) ==
Goal: find one process, agree yearly cost, book EUR 500 assessment on the call.
(opening, 3 min) Greeting, 20-sec Koref intro, agenda: how work runs, where time goes, honest verdict at end.
(how things run, 5 min) Team, systems, how an order moves. Listen for handoffs.
(where time goes, 10 min) Which task would you remove from the team tomorrow?
(cost) Volume, time per item, who does it, hourly cost. Calculate together.
(why it matters) Busy periods, holidays, growth, what team would do instead.
(decision, 5 min) Who else decides? Moment it matters most (busy season, vacancy)?
(close, 5 min) Play back process and cost. Ask permission for next step. Propose assessment. Book date, name staff. Mention mutual NDA. Ask decision maker at results meeting.
Before closing, check known: yearly cost, decision maker, timing.
Tone: Dutch SME owners. Plain, direct, calm, understated. No hype, no pressure. Leave silences.

== CORRECT WORDING FOR CLAIMS ==
Gartner: "Gartner expects over 40% of agentic AI projects to be cancelled by the end of 2027, citing rising costs, unclear value and weak risk controls." Not "because of token costs".
Cost vs closed models: "Our price is fixed up front, not per use."
Labour cost: ~EUR 48/hr Dutch average, fully loaded. Always "approximate average".
Outcome, correct: "Incoming orders are entered into Exact automatically by 1 March." Incorrect: "Around 40 hours of development."

== DON'TS ==
Never suggest hourly rate, day rate, or estimate in hours.
Never invent case studies, client names, results, statistics, or prices. No published case studies exist.
Never promise perfect accuracy or precision.
Never say "it pays for itself or it's free".
Never propose a build that does not pay back within 18 months.
Never claim OpenAI or other providers train on client data.
Never bring up OpenAI or competitors unless prospect raises them first.
Never say internal info out loud: pricing formula, EUR 1,000 fee, 5% referral fee, qualification criteria.
Never quote specific model retirement dates.
No legal or tax advice (EU AI Act, KIA deduction, contracts): suggest their own adviser or accountant.
No hype, no pressure tactics.
