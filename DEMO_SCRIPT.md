# Demo call script

A 4 minute discovery call for the live demo. The rep sells for Koref (the salesbook Girard loads). The prospect is Sanne, operations manager at Bakker Installatietechniek, an installation company with about 40 staff.

## Setup

- Two devices on one call (Google Meet, Teams or a phone call). The rep uses the laptop running Girard. The prospect talks from the other device.
- On the Girard laptop, pick the output the call plays on (usually the headset) under **Audio device**, then press **Start call**.
- Girard only hears the prospect. The rep's microphone is never captured.

## How to speak

- **Prospect:** speak each line as one clear sentence, then **pause about one second**. Girard reacts when the prospect stops talking.
- **Rep:** wait for the card, then answer in your own words, starting from the openers on the card.
- Don't talk over each other. Keep the prospect's lines close to the wording below; small changes are fine.

## The call

| # | Rep | Prospect | Card you should see |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hi Sanne, thanks for making time. | Hi, nice to meet you too. | none |
| 2 | The plan: understand how work runs and where the time goes. Which task would you take off your team tomorrow if you could? | Honestly, the work orders. Our planners type every order from email into Exact by hand. | Signal: documents typed into a system |
| 3 | How many per day? And how long does each one take? | About sixty a day. And we have an open vacancy for a planner, but we can't find anyone. | Signal: open vacancy |
| 4 | What would that role spend its time on? | Mostly this. But before we go further, what's your hourly rate? | Objection: asks hourly rate |
| 5 | We don't work by the hour. You pay a fixed price for a result, and delays are our cost. | Okay. And where would our customer data end up? | Answer: is our data safe? |
| 6 | Everything stays in the Netherlands, on open-source models. We sign an NDA before we start. | Good. One of our planners already uses ChatGPT to write emails, by the way. | Signal: staff use ChatGPT without a policy |
| 7 | Is there a policy for AI use at work? | Not really. We also had a quote from a company called Flowbase. | Competitor: unknown vendor (Flowbase) |
| 8 | What made you look at them, and what's missing for you today? | To be honest, our last IT supplier went way over budget. | Risk: bad experience with a supplier |
| 9 | What went wrong? With us, price and date are fixed before any code is written. | Five hundred euros just for an assessment? Others do that for free. | Objection: EUR 500 assessment |
| 10 | A free assessment is usually a sales talk. This is engineering time, and the report is yours either way. | I'd have to discuss this with my co-owner first. | Risk: needs to discuss with partner |
| 11 | What will your co-owner want to know? We could do a short call with both of you to go through the numbers. | That works. How fast could you actually start? | Answer: how long a build takes |
| 12 | We can plan the assessment this week. Shall we pick a date now? | Thursday works for me. | none |

Then press **End call** on the Girard laptop and show the debrief: summary, what went well, what to improve, customer needs, next steps and the follow-up email.

## If something goes wrong

- **No transcript appears:** the wrong audio device is selected, or it is muted. Go back, pick the output the call plays on, and start again.
- **A card is late or missing:** keep going. Girard never blocks the conversation, and the next line gets its own card.
- **"Audio capture stopped. Restarting."** Keep talking; capture comes back by itself within a second.
