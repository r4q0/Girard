# Advice examples: pick a style

The same 10 prospect lines (one Koref sales call, in order), each answered in 6 styles by zai-org/GLM-5.2.
Tell me which style(s) you like, per line or overall, and anything you'd change (length, tone, questions, numbers).

| Style | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| **A** | Three short bullets: Exactly 3 bullets, each 8 words or fewer. Telegraphic, no full sentences. |
| **B** | Two bullets + a question: 2 bullets of 12 words or fewer, then 1 bullet that is a question to ask, in quotes. |
| **C** | Say this + ask this: Exactly 2 bullets: first starts with 'Say:' and is one sentence the rep can say out loud; second starts with 'Ask:' and is one question in quotes. |
| **D** | Headline + two bullets: First bullet is a headline of what is really going on, 6 words or fewer, ending with a colon-free label like 'Price objection' or 'Buying signal'. Then 2 bullets of 12 words or fewer. |
| **E** | One point only: Exactly 1 bullet: the single most useful thing to do or say now, 15 words or fewer. |
| **F** | Numbers first: 2 or 3 bullets of 14 words or fewer. Whenever the prospect gives or implies numbers, work out the money (yearly cost, payback) and show it; otherwise give the one number they should ask for. |

## 1. “So basically we have three people who spend most of their morning typing incoming orders from email into Exact.”

**A · Three short bullets**
- 3 people, morning orders into Exact, quantify it
- Ask: minutes per order, how many daily
- Classic two-systems-not-talking: email → Exact

**B · Two bullets + a question**
- Three people typing emails into Exact every morning is exactly the kind of manual bridge we fix.
- That's likely well over EUR 10k/year of effort; a fixed-price build could remove most of it.
- How many orders per morning, and how many minutes does each one take to enter into Exact?

**C · Say this + ask this**
- Say: Three people typing orders from email into Exact every morning sounds like exactly the kind of manual, repetitive work that usually disappears with one connection.
- Ask: "Roughly how many orders a day come in by email, and how many minutes does each one take to type into Exact?"

**D · Headline + two bullets**
- Three people typing emails into Exact: clear pain
- Ask: how many orders per day, minutes per order
- Mention: workflow assessment costs per process and fastest payback

**E · One point only**
- Ask how many orders per morning and average minutes per order, you need both for the cost formula.

**F · Numbers first**
- 3 people × ~3 hrs/day × 230 days × ~EUR 48/hr avg = ~EUR 83,000/yr, strong case for a build
- Ask: how many orders per day, and minutes per order, to confirm the exact savings
- Mention: fixed price, 18-month payback guarantee, their risk is zero if it doesn't save
